
# Class 9 - Post Mortem Analysis

**Project:** Google Stadia  
**Company:** Google  
**Launched:** November 2019  
**Shut Down:** January 2023

## Project Overview

For this assignment, I chose Google Stadia, which was a gaming service that allowed people to play video games through the internet without needing a gaming console or an expensive computer. The idea was to make gaming easier and more accessible since people could play on different devices without having to download the games.

I chose this project because I thought it was interesting that a company as big as Google could come up with something like this and still have it fail. Stadia launched in 2019, but Google ended up shutting it down in January 2023 because it wasn't getting the number of users they had expected.

## What Went Wrong

I think one of the biggest problems with Stadia was that it didn't give people enough reasons to switch from the gaming platforms they were already using. Even though the technology was pretty impressive, a lot of people already had consoles or computers they were comfortable playing on.

Another problem was the games available on Stadia. It didn't have enough of the popular games or exclusive titles that would make people want to try something different. I think this made it harder for the service to compete with other gaming platforms.

Google also decided to shut down its own game development studios in 2021, which meant it wouldn't be making its own games for Stadia anymore. To me, this seems like something that made the platform even more dependent on other developers.

## Impact of the Failure

When Google announced that Stadia was shutting down, people who had purchased games and equipment were affected because they would no longer be able to use the service. Google did offer refunds for eligible purchases, which I think was important since customers had already spent money on the platform.

The shutdown also affected the people who had worked on the project, including developers and employees. Even though Stadia itself didn't work out, Google planned to use some of the technology for other purposes.

## Lessons Learned

One thing I learned from looking into Stadia is that just because a company has a good idea and the resources to build it doesn't mean people will actually use it. I think it's important to understand what customers want and whether there's enough demand before putting so much time and money into a project.

I also think Stadia shows why it's important to keep checking how a project is performing. If something isn't working the way it was expected to, the team needs to figure out what's causing the problem and make changes before it gets worse.

Another thing I noticed was how important it is to have something that makes a product stand out. Stadia was different because of how it worked, but that wasn't enough to convince people to leave the platforms they were already using.

## Recommendations

If I were managing a project like Stadia, I would try to get more feedback from potential users early on to see what would actually make them interested in using the service. I would also focus on having a better selection of games before launching, since that's one of the main things people look for in a gaming platform.

I would also keep track of how many people were using the platform and what problems they were having. That way, the team could make changes based on actual feedback instead of waiting until the project was already struggling.

Overall, I think Google Stadia is a good example of how even a large company can have a project fail. It helped me understand that planning, customer feedback, and keeping track of progress are just as important as the technology itself.
